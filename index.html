1. Nature and Independence of the Study

You are invited to take part in an independent academic research study conducted by Jibran Bugvi, a doctoral candidate at the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT). This study seeks to explore the relationship between usability metrics, consumer perceptions and perceived financial flexibility through the lens of Agoda’s Pay-Later feature, where customers make a travel booking but pay for the purchase at a later date rather than at the time of the booking.

Instructions: Please answer all questions based on a specific travel booking you made on the Agoda platform in the last 120 days using the Pay-Later option only.

IMPORTANT NOTICE REGARDING AGODA: THIS STUDY IS AN ENTIRELY INDEPENDENT ENDEAVOUR OF THE RESEARCHER AND IS NOT COMMISSIONED, SPONSORED, OPERATED, FUNDED, AFFILIATED OR ENDORSED BY AGODA COMPANY PTE. While the Researcher is employed by Agoda, this academic project is conducted strictly in his personal capacity as a doctoral student. Your participation is completely voluntary and will have NO IMPACT on your Agoda account, customer status, bookings, loyalty benefits, or relationship with Agoda.

2. Eligibility Criteria & Geographic Residency Confirmation

Participation is strictly limited to individuals who meet ALL of the following criteria:

  • You are at least twenty one (21) years of age;
  • You currently reside in any Southeast Asian (ASEAN) nation; and
  • You personally completed a past accommodation booking on Agoda in the last 120 days using the Pay-Later option.

GEOGRAPHIC EXCLUSION: RESIDENTS AND CITIZENS OF THE EUROPEAN ECONOMIC AREA (EEA), THE UNITED KINGDOM, SWITZERLAND, AND THE UNITED STATES ARE STRICTLY INELIGIBLE TO PARTICIPATE. By proceeding, you confirm that you reside in Southeast Asia and are NOT a resident of the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or US.

3. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw

Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may exit the survey at any time prior to clicking "Submit" without consequence. Once your submission is received and your corresponding data has been de-identified and incorporated into the research dataset, it may no longer be possible to isolate, edit, or delete your individual responses.

4. Agoda Booking ID & Server-Side Data Extraction Authorization

For the purposes of this study, you will be asked to enter a 10-digit Booking ID from a past Agoda booking that you personally made and completed using the Pay-Later option in the last 120 days. By providing this Booking ID, you expressly authorize the Researcher to request, and Agoda Company Pte. Ltd. to extract and disclose to the Researcher, the following three (3) server-side numerical usability metrics corresponding to that specific Booking ID:

  1. Number of clicks — the aggregate count of user clicks during the booking funnel (e.g., 12 clicks);
  2. Task duration — elapsed time in seconds taken to complete the booking funnel (e.g., 105 seconds); and
  3. Task deviation count — aggregate number of repeated steps, selection changes, backtracking events (e.g., 3 counts).

Matching is conducted strictly on Booking ID and your email address will NOT be shared with Agoda.

Privacy Notice

This notice summarises how your personal data is handled. The consent form above contains the full legal terms; where it refers to email addresses, please note that this survey does not collect email addresses at all.

Data collected: your full name and 10-digit Booking ID (for the legal consent record and verification of your authorization), together with your survey answers (including age and gender). No email address is collected.

Use: your name and Booking ID verify your consent under a Data Sharing Agreement with the platform; the Booking ID authorizes one-time extraction of three numerical usability metrics (clicks, task duration, task deviation) for that booking. Analysis is performed on de-identified data only; all published outputs contain aggregated statistics only.

Lawful basis: your explicit informed consent, given before any data is collected. Participation is voluntary and you may withdraw before submitting, without consequence.

Retention: identifiers (name, Booking ID) are stored separately from research data and destroyed within approximately 12 months, once verification is complete; de-identified research data is permanently deleted within five (5) years of study completion.

Storage & security: data is stored in the Researcher’s private Google account, encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by two-factor authentication, with access restricted to the Researcher only. This survey website itself stores nothing.

Sharing: the platform receives only your consent record (name + Booking ID) for verification — never your survey answers. Google provides hosting infrastructure only. No other third party receives your data.

Your rights: before your data is de-identified, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing st122430@ait.asia.

Standards: this study follows the Asian Institute of Technology’s research ethics and data-protection requirements and the principles of the Thai Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Residents of the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and the US are excluded from this study by design.
