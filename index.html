1. Consent Form
Details of Study: This academic research project at the Asian Institute of Technology on Embedded Finance is aimed at exploring the relationship between usability metrics (clicks, task duration, task deviation), consumer perceptions and perceived financial flexibility.

Instructions: Please answer all questions based on a specific travel booking you made on the Agoda platform in the last 60 days using the Pay-Later option.

* Indicates required question.

Taking Part in the Study

I have read and understood the study information. I have had the opportunity to contact the research team with any questions about the study. My participation is voluntary. I may stop the survey at any time before submitting my response without giving a reason. After my data have been de-identified and incorporated into the research dataset, it may no longer be possible to identify and remove my individual response. I understand that the researchers conducting this study may be employed by Agoda. My participation is voluntary and will not affect my Agoda account or relationship with Agoda.


I understand that taking part in this study will require me to share a unique Booking ID from a past booking I made on Agoda and I provide permission to the research team to approach Agoda for the following server-side data for the Booking ID I provide, for Agoda to release that information to the research team and for it to be utilized for this study if Agoda consents:

  1. Number of clicks — data used to determine the number of clicks made during the booking process.
  2. Task duration — timestamp data used to calculate the time taken to complete the booking process.
  3. Task deviation — data used to identify repeated steps, changes in selections, backtracking, or other navigation during the booking process and whether Pay-Later and non-Pay-Later options were both visible in the session.

The Booking ID and, if required for matching, the email address associated with the booking may be used solely for this matching process. Identifying information will not appear in research outputs or publications.


Use of Information in the Study

I understand the data I provide (the questionnaire response below and the permitted extraction of server side data mentioned above) will be used for academic research purposes and possibly published in an academic journal. I understand that personal data collected about me that can identify me will not be shared beyond the study team. Confidentiality will be protected in this manner and data will be stored securely on Google cloud and access restricted. If offline versions are extracted, datasets used for analysis will be de-identified or pseudonymized for confidentiality. Data will not be retained beyond a period of 5-years from completion of the study.
